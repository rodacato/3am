# ADR 0001: The window direction, with printed slips

Status: accepted, 2026-10-16

## Context

Step 0 produced three directions of the same moment: the night desk from the brief, a top-down blotter where every query prints a slip, and a window where the city skyline is the service map. See `design/step0/`.

## Decision

Build on the window: evidence is taped to the glass, hypotheses are strung to the building they accuse, and the skyline's lit windows and cables show service health live. From the blotter we keep the printed slip: every query leaves a receipt in a tray with its tool, clock and cost, and evidence is torn off results as paper slips.

## Consequences

Dawn is literal (the sky over the skyline brightens with incident time). Day shift (case 7) can show uninstrumented services as dark buildings. The service map stops being a separate tool; it is always in view. A skyline only holds about a dozen components, which bounds case topology size.
